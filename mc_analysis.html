Motor Cortex · Deep Analysis

GO Enrichment & FTD/AD Associations

Gene Ontology enrichment for ALS-filtered modules and modules significantly associated with FTD/AD comorbidity in the motor cortex.

Gene Ontology Enrichment

Biological Process & Cellular Component

GO enrichment was run on all genes in each ALS-filtered module (ALS p<0.05, RIN p>0.05) using clusterProfiler with BH multiple testing correction. Only terms with adjusted p<0.05 are shown. Top 12 terms per module are displayed. Gene IDs were mapped from Ensembl to Entrez before enrichment.

MC — ALS Filtered Modules · BP + CC

GO enrichment for all 11 ALS-filtered motor cortex modules. The motor cortex shows bidirectional changes — DOWN modules (synaptic/cholinergic biology) and UP modules (RNA processing, stress response).

FTD/AD Association Analysis

Modules Linked to Neurodegeneration Comorbidity

This heatmap shows all modules with a significant FTD/AD comorbidity association (p<0.05). Unlike the ALS-filtered heatmap, ALS significance is not required here — the filter is purely FTD/AD p<0.05. This reveals which motor cortex gene programmes are specifically altered in ALS patients with FTD/AD comorbidity.

MC — FTD/AD Significant Modules (FTD/AD p < 0.05)

Only 2 motor cortex modules pass FTD/AD p<0.05, indicating the motor cortex transcriptome is less reactive to FTD/AD comorbidity than the frontal cortex — perhaps reflecting that motor cortex pathology in ALS is driven predominantly by disease mechanism rather than concurrent AD/FTD tau pathology.
